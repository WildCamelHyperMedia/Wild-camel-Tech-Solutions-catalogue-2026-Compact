The content comes from us

Every installation in this catalogue runs on content: the films on the screens, the figures in the holograms, the 3D built for anamorphic corners, the projection, the AR, the games and the sound. Our own studio designs, directs and produces all of it, so the installation and what plays on it are made together, by one team, under one creative direction.

  • Creative direction
  • Screen content
  • Holographic display content
  • 3D for anamorphic screens
  • Projection mapping content
  • AR, VR & game content
  • Film, CGI & AI film
  • Photography & key visuals
  • Sound, music & voice
