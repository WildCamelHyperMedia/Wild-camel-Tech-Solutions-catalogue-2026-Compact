# Wild Camel — Activations

Ideas for spaces, events and screens: 191 physical, hybrid and digital activations by Wild Camel, Dubai. One picture and one line each, with the moment it creates, what it is best for, its scale and a typical lead time.

**Open the catalogue:** https://wildcamelhypermedia.github.io/Wild-camel-Tech-Solutions-catalogue-2026-Compact/

## How to use it

- **Browse** by family — Physical (things that move), Hybrid (objects that think), Digital (software that travels) — and by category, or filter by scale, lead time and status. The search box finds ideas by name, place or theme.
- **Open any card** for the full picture: the moment, where it works best, scale, lead time, and the client it was delivered for where that applies.
- **Shortlist** the ideas you like with the + on each card. *Email to Wild Camel* sends the list to us; *Copy share link* gives you an address that opens the same shortlist for a colleague.
- **Share views.** Every filtered view and every idea has its own address, so a link can be sent as it is — for example `#H-85` opens The Transformation Mirror.

## Good to know

- Every idea carries a code (P-, H- or D- and a number). Quote the code when you write to us and we will know exactly which one you mean.
- The catalogue is made to adapt: each idea can be dressed for your theme, venue and audience, and most can be combined.
- Lead times are typical and for planning; they are confirmed once the brief is known.
- The content the installations run on — films, holographic and anamorphic 3D, projection, games, sound — is designed, directed and produced by our own studio.
- All pictures are concept visuals.
